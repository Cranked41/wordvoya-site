Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the Wordvoya: Word Puzzle Android app (com.cranked.wordvoya) and this website.

In short: Wordvoya does not collect personal data. It needs no account and has no internet permission; your game data is stored only on your device.

Data We Collect

  • No account: The app does not ask you to sign up or sign in, and it does not receive your name, email address or any other identifier.
  • No internet: The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data from your device to us or anyone else.
  • Game data on your device: Level progress, found words, the Word Notebook, the review schedule, the daily streak, coins and settings are stored only on your device.

How the Data Is Used

The data on your device is used only to run the game: to open your current level, add the words you learn to your notebook and ask you the words that are due for review. It is never sent to us or to third parties and is never sold. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play: The app is distributed through Google Play. Google's own privacy policy applies to downloads and updates.
  • Android backup: If Android backup is turned on, the system may include your game data in your Google account backup and restore it on a new device. Google manages that backup; we have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

The app uses no other third-party services.

This Website

This site does not use cookies and loads no third-party resources (fonts, analytics or ads). Our server may keep standard access logs (IP address, browser information, requested page, time) for a short period for security and troubleshooting; they are not used for anything else.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your game data stays on your device. To delete it, uninstall the app or use Settings > Data > Reset progress in the app. We keep no account or game records about you on our servers, so there is nothing else to delete.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and the "last updated" date is changed. If a feature that processes data (for example, ads) is added to the app, this page will be updated before that feature is released.

Contact

For questions: support@wordvoya.com
